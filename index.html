COA Reports

How to read a report

Batch number
The lot code printed on the vial label. Reports are issued per batch, so match the code exactly before relying on a result.
Purity
Usually measured by HPLC. It is the target compound's share of everything detected in the sample, as a percentage. It says how clean the material is, not how much is in the vial.
Content
The milligrams of compound the lab measured in the vial, shown next to the amount on the label.
Endotoxin
A check for bacterial endotoxins, usually by LAL assay and reported in endotoxin units (EU). Pass means the result was under the limit stated on the lab's document.
Sterility
The sample is incubated in growth media to see whether anything grows. Pass means no growth was observed.
TFA
Trifluoroacetic acid is used in peptide synthesis and purification. This test reports how much of it remains.
